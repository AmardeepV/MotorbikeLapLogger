Bluetooth battery-powered capture is a useful next test-environment improvement. Existing iPhone app already has service/command/status/lean characteristics but its T1 payload lacks pitch and validity, and it does not persist raw bench recordings. A wireless test requires firmware startup/control without serial, a versioned bench protocol, disconnect/stale handling, and a receiver that can save/export records. Existing standalone sensor firmware does not currently advertise the app BLE service. Merely unplugging USB will not enable wireless capture. No firmware or app change made during this analysis.
